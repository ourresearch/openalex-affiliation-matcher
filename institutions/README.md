# What changed for each institution

One directory per institution: `README.md` says what changed and why, `removed.csv` lists the affiliation strings that no longer link to the institution, and `added.csv` the ones that now do (gzipped when over 1 MB). Measured on the live data on 28 September 2026, against the assignments just before the switch.

For now these are the institutions listed on our [institutional supporters](https://openalex.org/institutional-supporters) page, and the University of California campuses. Want one for another institution? [Tell us](https://openalex.org/contact).

Thank you to the librarians and curators whose corrections showed us where the old matcher failed and helped us check the new one. Their corrections still override the matcher.
